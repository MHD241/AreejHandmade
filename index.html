THE STORY

Made slowly, on purpose

Areej Handmade is a small studio. Each hoop, bag and patch is designed, stitched and finished by one pair of hands, with cotton thread and natural fabrics where possible.

Because everything is made to order, you will wait a little. What arrives is a piece that was made for you, and not one of a thousand on a shelf.

How ordering works

  1. ChooseAdd pieces to your basket, or send a custom request.
  2. Send your orderOne tap sends it to Areej on WhatsApp or by email.
  3. Confirm and payAreej checks the details, confirms delivery and sends payment details.
  4. Wait for the postYour piece is stitched, wrapped and sent.
